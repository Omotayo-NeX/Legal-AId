Q: What is Legal AI.d?
A: Legal AI.d is an AI-powered legal assistant specializing in Nigerian tax laws, particularly the Tax Reform Acts 2025-2026. It helps individuals and businesses understand tax regulations, calculate taxes, and ensure compliance.
Q: Is Legal AI.d free to use?
A: Yes! Legal AI.d offers free tools including the 2026 tax calculator and AI-powered tax queries. We're committed to making tax information accessible to everyone in Nigeria.
Q: What tax reforms does Legal AI.d cover?
A: Legal AI.d covers the Nigerian Tax Reform Acts 2025-2026, including PAYE, VAT, digital asset taxation, corporate tax, and individual income tax regulations under the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS).
