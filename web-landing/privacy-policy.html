Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Last Updated: December 16, 2024

Welcome to Legal AI.d. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Profile Data: User preferences, settings, and subscription tier
  • Communication Data: Your questions, legal queries, and conversations with our AI assistant
  • Document Data: Information you provide for document generation
  • Tax Information: Data entered into tax calculators (we do not store sensitive financial information)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage, session duration
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, error logs

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect payment information directly (handled by secure third-party processors)
  • We do not access your device contacts, photos, or other unrelated data
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: Deliver AI-powered legal and tax assistance
  • Generate Responses: Process your queries through OpenAI's API to provide accurate answers based on Nigerian law
  • Document Generation: Create legal documents based on your specifications
  • Compliance Reminders: Send notifications about tax deadlines and legal obligations
  • Improve Services: Analyze usage patterns to enhance app functionality
  • Account Management: Authenticate users, manage subscriptions, provide support
  • Communication: Send service updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with Nigerian laws and regulations

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Where We Store Your Data

  • Supabase: User accounts, profiles, and app data (encrypted at rest)
  • Railway: Backend API infrastructure (secure cloud hosting)
  • OpenAI: Your queries are processed through OpenAI's API for AI responses (subject to OpenAI's data policies)

3.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encrypted database storage
  • Secure authentication using Supabase Auth
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and permission management

3.3 Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Personal data deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Chat History: Stored until you manually delete or close your account
  • Legal Obligations: Some data may be retained longer if required by Nigerian law

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect and process your data:

4.1 OpenAI API

  • Processes your legal and tax queries to generate AI responses
  • OpenAI does not use API data to train their models (as per their data usage policy)
  • Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy

4.2 Supabase

  • Provides authentication and database services
  • GDPR-compliant with SOC 2 Type II certification
  • Subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy

4.3 Railway

  • Hosts our backend API infrastructure
  • Secure cloud hosting with enterprise-grade security

5. Your Rights and Choices

Under Nigerian data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Disable non-essential notifications and analytics
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights, access your account settings in the app or contact us at privacy@legalaid.ng.

6. Children's Privacy

Legal AI.d is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share information
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist in providing our services (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by Nigerian law, court order, or government request
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you will be notified)
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, safety, or property, or that of our users

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored outside Nigeria, including in the United States and European Union, where our service providers operate. We ensure that adequate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with international standards.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Our app does not use traditional cookies. However, we may use:

  • Local Storage: To remember your preferences and authentication state
  • Analytics: Anonymized usage statistics to improve our services

You can manage these settings in the app's privacy settings section.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notification
  • Email to your registered address
  • Updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of Legal AI.d after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Legal Basis for Processing (Nigerian Data Protection)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), we process your data based on:

  • Consent: You agree to this Privacy Policy when creating an account
  • Contract Performance: To provide the services you request
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our services and ensure security
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with Nigerian laws and regulations
